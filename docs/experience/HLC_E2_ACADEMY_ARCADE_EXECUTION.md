## Trust and anti-gaming boundaries

- XP is progress currency, not a trust score.
- Community popularity does not prove competency.
- Event attendance does not create certification.
- HLC certifications must remain distinct from external licenses/credentials.
- Application XP requires a verified outcome rather than a click or self-asserted completion.
- Repeat attempts may provide limited learning credit but cannot be farmed indefinitely.
- Certification requires assessment identity, threshold, score, date, and teacher evidence.

## Current evidence boundary

The source foundation defines canonical routes, role tracks, teacher ownership, progression states, XP award limits, and certification evidence rules without fabricating runtime persistence.
