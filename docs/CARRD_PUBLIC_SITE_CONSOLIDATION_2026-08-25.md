# HLC Carrd Public-Site Consolidation

Date: 2026-08-25
Status: LOCKED

Carrd is the public marketing/legal layer. The authenticated Vite/Supabase application owns operational CRM, matching, jobs, community, communications, resources, portals, settings and billing.

## Rule

Keep exactly ten Carrd sites. Do not create a new Carrd site for a logged-in feature. Public pages may explain or preview application features but must link into the application for actual work.

## 1. Home
URL: https://homeleadconnect.org/

Owns:
- strongest HLC overview
- primary resident/professional CTA
- visual product story
- estimator/match/schedule/message preview
- professional workspace preview
- AI team preview
- Golden Workflow/service journey preview

Does not own:
- live CRM
- provider directory
- live map
- account settings
- billing management
- community feed

## 2. About
URL: https://homeleadconnectabout.carrd.co/

Owns:
- mission
- why HLC exists
- residents/renters/homeowners/professionals served
- Harrisburg/local/community story where appropriate
- company/brand story

Does not duplicate Platform feature catalogs.

## 3. Platform
URL: https://homeleadconnectplatform.carrd.co/

Owns public explanation/previews of:
- requests/leads
- estimator
- matching
- provider network
- map
- scheduling
- jobs
- communications
- workflow
- community
- professional tools

Every live feature CTA goes to the authenticated application or onboarding flow.

## 4. Innovation
URL: https://homeleadconnectinnovation.carrd.co/

Owns:
- Kendrell
- Dion
- Diamond
- automation strategy
- connected systems
- intelligence
- future-facing platform concepts

Does not become a duplicate AI command center.

## 5. Professionals
URL: https://homeleadconnectprofessionals.carrd.co/

Owns:
- professional value proposition
- opportunity flow
- provider profile preview
- service-area/availability preview
- jobs/calendar/reviews/performance preview
- professional CTA/onboarding

Actual professional work remains in `/contractor-portal` and authenticated workspace routes.

## 6. Welcome
URL: https://homeleadconnectwelcome.carrd.co/

Owns:
- audience choice
- simple onboarding explanation
- resident / professional / partner routing
- request-service and sign-in paths

Keep intentionally short.

## 7. Demo
URL: https://homeleadconnectdemo.carrd.co/

Owns:
- visual product tour
- why a demo matters
- demo/request CTA
- a small number of representative screenshots

Does not repeat the full Platform page.

## 8. Contact
URL: https://contacthomeleadconnect.carrd.co/

Owns:
- low-friction contact options
- general inquiry routing
- support/contact expectations

Do not place full Help Center content here.

## 9. Privacy
URL: https://homeleadconnectprivacy.carrd.co/

Owns only approved privacy-policy content. Do not invent policy language.

## 10. Terms
URL: https://homeleadconnectterms.carrd.co/

Owns only approved terms-of-use content. Do not invent legal provisions.
