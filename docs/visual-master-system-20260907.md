# HomeLead Connect — Approved Visual Master System

Status: implementation authority for the post-launch visual cleanup branch.

## Scope

Visual structure only. Do not change business logic, Supabase behavior, RLS, authentication, billing, routes, roles, permissions, workflow rules, lead/job lifecycle semantics, or AI agent ownership.

## Core rules

- Operational pages use a compact eyebrow + page title + at most one compact primary action.
- No marketing/pitch copy or oversized intro cards inside recurring operational workspaces.
- Auth is an account-entry surface: dark HomeLead Connect canvas, one job, no competing marketing banner.
- Prefer continuous dark premium surfaces, rows and hairline dividers over nested card walls.
- Do not use white, cream, eggwhite, or off-white as the default authenticated canvas or as large visual islands inside dark authenticated workspaces.
- Dark premium does not mean every page must use the same navy treatment. Product areas may use restrained section and department accents when those accents clarify purpose.
- Purple/indigo is an accent, not the app-wide canvas or master brand identity. Preserve HomeLead Connect navy/blue ownership and use richer accents deliberately rather than washing whole pages in purple.
- KPI summaries are number-first and compact; decorative icons are not required.
- Dense list rows prioritize identity, status/value/timestamp, and next action. Full contact/detail data belongs in detail views.
- Secondary row actions are compact 44px touch targets; overflow is an icon, not a full-width button.
- Empty states collapse to concise text rather than large whitespace cards.
- Mobile uses progressive list-to-detail navigation and preserves 44px touch targets.
- Mobile business primary navigation remains Home · Work · Community · Messages · More; secondary destinations belong under More or contextual navigation according to role.
- Messages is conversation-first. On mobile show the conversation list first; opening a conversation uses the screen as the conversation space. Do not add decorative dashboard heroes to Messages.
- Community may feel warmer and more human/local than Work while remaining part of the same HomeLead Connect system.
- Academy should read as learning/practice, not as another CRM dashboard.
- Contextual AI remains subordinate to the work surface and stays clear of bottom navigation, list rows and composers. Kendrell, Dion and Diamond keep their locked identities and department roles.
- Use the existing HomeLead Connect navy/blue foundation plus approved supporting accents. Do not invent a replacement palette.
- Raw database strings should not become user-facing visual labels without human formatting.
- Prefer source/component-root corrections over additional blanket terminal-cascade patches when the underlying component structure is the demonstrated defect.
