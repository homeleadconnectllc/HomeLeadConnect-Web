## Non-negotiable asymmetry

Resident → Professional permission does not imply Professional → Resident solicitation permission.

Community profile visibility does not imply phone/email visibility.

Referral receipt does not imply CRM access.

QR scan does not prove identity, consent or authority.

Saving/following does not create a private relationship.

A profile view does not create messaging permission.

An advertisement does not create consent.

An internal role may still require separate approve, sign, bill, export or automation authority.
