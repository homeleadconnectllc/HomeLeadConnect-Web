# HomeLead Connect — Active Build Governing Addendum

Status: ACTIVE IMMEDIATELY  
Effect: clarifies authority and guardrails; does not restart, reset, or invalidate correctly completed work.

## Permanent whole-system execution rule

Every sprint is governed by `HCX_GLOBAL_WHOLE_SYSTEM_EXECUTION_RULE.md` at task, affected-system, and bounded application-impact scope. A reported page or file is an entry point, not automatically the whole defect or the whole solution. Determine root cause, ownership layer, blast radius, canonical repair, and affected-surface verification without widening into unrelated redesign.

## Autonomous execution
Continue approved E2E milestones without routine owner approval for bounded, reversible, non-production work already resolved by HomeLead Connect authority. Investigate, reconcile, implement, refactor where necessary, repair, test/retest, document, commit and push appropriate non-production work.

Autonomy does not authorize invention of business rules, final pricing, legal terms, permissions, public branding, or consequential legal/financial/signature/security/ownership/billing/permission authority.

Use established authority instead of re-asking. For genuinely unspecified safe implementation details, make the strongest evidence-backed engineering/product decision, record it and continue. Owner-only matters enter the Exception Ledger while independent work continues.

## Production
Production promotion requires owner approval of the exact certified candidate SHA. Earlier approval of a concept, branch, milestone or different SHA is not promotion approval. Protect verified production during candidate work.

## Consolidation
Do not start over. Preserve working implementation and useful CRM/SaaS/automation/workflow/visual/mobile capability. Historical work is reconciliation evidence, not authority to restore superseded architecture. Do not create competing systems where consolidation is viable. There is one authoritative repository.

## Product principle
**Simple on the surface. Comprehensive, governed, and automated underneath.**

User-facing priority: goal → current state → appropriate next action → genuine attention. Keep permission, entitlement, consent, workflow, retry, synchronization, provenance, audit, routing, AI and billing machinery beneath progressive disclosure.

Use smart defaults, contextual actions, inline editing, popovers, drawers, bottom sheets, guided flows, automation, saved preferences, prefill, permission-aware controls and concise state communication.

## Communication-first principle
Phone, phone number, email, SMS, contact identity, qualification, scheduling and CRM history are priority infrastructure.

Optimize: find correct person → understand context → call/text/email → qualify → schedule/update appointment → record result → trigger correct next action.

Reuse known data. Associate communications with governed record/history. No-response, reschedule, cancellation, reminders, re-engagement and escalation are first-class journeys.

## Automation and AI boundary
Automation/AI may take safe, reversible, pre-authorized actions and reduce waiting/repetition. They may not manufacture authority or independently create consequential legal, financial, signature, billing, security, ownership or permission decisions requiring an authorized human.

On expired human response: remind → safely continue where pre-authorized → reassign where permitted → escalate → preserve audit history.

## Personalization
Always enforce HomeLead Connect system rule → organization rule → role permission → entitlement/capability → contextual scope → individual preference. Personalization cannot expand authority or weaken higher rules.

## Public identity
HCX is internal shorthand. External identity is HomeLead Connect; HomeLead Connect LLC where legally required. Do not leak HCX into public branding, documents, marketing, social identity, QR destinations or product naming absent explicit exception. Use only the governed official circular master badge.

## Visual owner checkpoints
Owner governs overall visual character without becoming a page-by-page dependency.

- **Checkpoint A — Direction Lock:** representative public, workspace, CRM/record, communication/scheduling and iPhone experiences.
- **Checkpoint B — System Review:** major families across desktop/mobile.
- **Checkpoint C — Final Candidate:** exact certified candidate on approved HomeLead Connect domains.

Routine spacing, breakpoints, responsive adaptation, states, crops, accessibility corrections, menus/sheets/drawers and governed implementation details do not require owner approval. Systemic visual feedback is repaired at system level. Independent work continues around checkpoints.

## Visual principle
Do not turn HomeLead Connect into generic SaaS. Preserve the softer/misted coherent family and pathway identities. Avoid card walls, excessive boxes/buttons, harsh two-tone blocks, unnecessary glow/motion, generic/artificial imagery, wasted space and squeezed desktop layouts. Motion communicates state/hierarchy/response/attention. Operational density is permitted when organized and comprehensible.

## Completion quality
Neither beautiful dead controls nor ugly-but-functional screens are complete. Every visible control works, navigates, exposes a truthful permission-aware disabled state, or is intentionally explained. Verify through rendering and interaction in addition to source/tests.

## Coverage Register
Every discovered route, page, setting, menu action, component, record type, workflow, trigger, automation, communication journey, document type, report, entitlement, integration, mobile behavior and meaningful legacy capability receives a disposition:

implemented; retained; consolidated; repaired; intentionally retired with reason; superseded with authority; deferred with reason; blocked; owner decision required.

“Not noticed” is not a disposition.

## No fake completion
Where applicable completion requires implementation → rendering → interaction → state/failure testing → responsive verification → permission/entitlement verification → repair → retest → evidence. A page, menu item, compile, mockup, document, single happy path or isolated passing test is insufficient by itself.

## Speed
Compress duplicate effort; do not skip necessary work. Prefer shared primitives/page families/tokens/workflows, centralized permission/entitlement/pricing/communications/document infrastructure, automation, parallel independent work and system-level fixes. Do not reopen settled questions or substitute speculative documentation for the next useful implementation/test action.

## Owner interruption
Owner involvement is reserved for the three visual checkpoints, genuine owner-authority exceptions blocking a relevant decision, and final exact-candidate promotion approval. Otherwise: investigate → decide within authority → implement → test → repair → continue.
