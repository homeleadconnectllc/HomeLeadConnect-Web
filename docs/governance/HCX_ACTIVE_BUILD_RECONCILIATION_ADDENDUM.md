# HomeLead Connect — Active-Build Reconciliation Addendum

Status: ACTIVE BUILD AUTHORITY  
Effect: continue the current build; do not restart or replace newer architecture with historical implementations.

## Reconciliation rule
As each system area is implemented, reconcile it against complete current HomeLead Connect authority and relevant historical CRM/SaaS, automation, Lead Vacuum, Base44, Supabase, communications, appointments, AI/voice, OAuth and migration evidence. Current authority wins every conflict. Preserve useful capability, not necessarily its old implementation.

Every discovered historical capability is classified as: implemented/current; implemented but disconnected; partially implemented; duplicated; superseded; planned only; missing but still required.

## Communication identity — critical E2E
Treat phone, SMS/text, email, calls, voicemail/voice and communication history as connected parts of governed person/organization identity.

Verify the lifecycle:
**Resident/service request → identity/contact → lead/opportunity → professional/provider → communication → appointment → job/work → completion → follow-up → permanent history.**

Name, phone and email support reliable matching/deduplication without unsafe merges.

Where applicable every communication resolves sender, recipient, related person/contact, organization/workspace, portal/role context, related request/lead/job/appointment, channel, consent/preference state, delivery/status history, timestamp, responsible automation/workflow and audit evidence.

Users must not reconstruct history manually across separate phone, email, CRM and appointment systems.

## Visible-control integrity
Every dashboard item, setting, option, button, status, filter, shortcut, notification, report, automation, AI action, communication control and administrative control maps to:

**data source → permission/entitlement → action/service → resulting state → history/audit trail**

No decorative controls, dead settings, fake metrics, placeholder workflows or disconnected UI survive final certification.

## Personalization boundary
Apply globally:
**HCX system rule → organization rule → role permission → entitlement/capability → contextual scope → individual preference.**

Personalization may affect presentation, convenience, permitted notifications/privacy, AI preferences, shortcuts, saved views, accessibility and personal operating behavior. It never weakens security/legal requirements, organization rules, permissions, billing/ownership authority, immutable records, consent or audit/history. Clearly identify Personal, Organization and HCX Managed controls.

## Entitlements and records
For every meaningful page, feature, record, workflow, report, automation, download, AI action and communication capability resolve ownership; actors; view/edit/approve authority; entitlement; storage; export; retention; consent/agreement; automation; AI; advertising eligibility where applicable; legal/audit requirements; source of truth; and downgrade/cancellation lifecycle. These are architecture requirements now, not deferred pricing work.

## Completion evidence
Before E2E completion, reconcile:
**historical capability → current capability → superseded → repaired/reconnected → intentionally retired → unresolved**

Routine reconciliation findings are repaired under existing authority and included in final evidence. Escalate only genuine owner/legal/business decisions or exact-production-candidate approval.
