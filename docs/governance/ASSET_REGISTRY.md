# HomeLead Connect — Asset Registry

## Official Brand Assets
Canonical HomeLead artwork must remain separate from generated scene art. The approved full logo, circular HLC mark, and standalone emblem are the source assets for official branding.

Locked official source:
- `/public/brand/homelead-connect-official-master.svg`

Never alter, redraw, approximate, retype, or restyle the official logo for production/prototype brand surfaces. Generated scenes may include environmental signage only when the official logo is placed from the locked source asset or a verified derivative.

## Canonical AI Persona Assets
- Kendrell — locked canonical portrait
- Dion — locked canonical portrait
- Diamond — locked canonical portrait

Production currently references persona imagery through the HomeLead brand asset paths, including `/brand/avatars/Kendrell_Locked_HLC.png`, `/brand/avatars/Dion_Locked_HLC.png`, and `/brand/avatars/Diamond_Locked_HLC.png` where available.

Locked identity source portraits:
- Kendrell — `/public/brand/avatars/sources/Kendrell_Identity_Source_20260920.jpeg`
- Dion — `/public/brand/avatars/sources/Dion_Identity_Source_20260920.jpeg`
- Diamond — `/public/brand/avatars/sources/Diamond_Identity_Source_20260920.jpeg`

Never alter agent facial identity. Solo and team/together imagery may change pose, crop, setting, lighting, clothing, and workplace context only when the face remains the locked identity. Generated or edited images that drift facial structure, age, skin tone, eye shape, mouth shape, hairline, or recognizable likeness must not replace the locked assets.

## Supporting Variants
Supporting portraits may change pose, crop, workplace, lighting, departmental screens, wardrobe, and environmental context. They must be registered as supporting assets and do not replace canonical portraits automatically. Together/team images must use the same locked faces for Kendrell, Dion, and Diamond.

## Governance
- Keep canonical persona images separate.
- Keep official HLC logos as separate overlay/source artwork.
- AI-generated logo approximations do not become production brand assets.
- Canonical replacement requires explicit approval.
- Canonical face replacement requires explicit approval and a hash update in the locked identity tests.
