# HomeLead Connect — Complete Product Authority & Build Supplement

Status: ACTIVE PRODUCT AUTHORITY  
Effect: supplement the active E2E build; do not restart, invalidate correct work, or restore superseded implementations.

This document preserves complete product intent during consolidation. Current explicit owner authority wins conflicts. Historical capability is reconciliation evidence.

## 1. Identity and north star
Public identity is **HomeLead Connect**; use **HomeLead Connect LLC** where legally required. **HCX is internal shorthand only.**

HomeLead Connect is one connected operating experience, not separate lead-marketplace, directory, CRM, scheduling, website, community, document, AI or SaaS products.

Core journey:
**Resident need → identity → request → qualification → opportunity → professional/provider → communication → appointment → work/job → documents → completion → follow-up → re-engagement → reporting → permanent history.**

North star: sophisticated machinery underneath without making users feel that complexity. Residents get help without learning CRM; professionals work opportunities without fighting software; teams know what needs attention; communications remain attached to identity/history; appointments continue the journey; documents remain trustworthy; automation keeps work moving without inventing authority; AI assists rather than controls; mobile is first class; public and authenticated experiences unmistakably belong to the same company.

## 2. Experience model
**Simple on the surface. Comprehensive underneath.**

Every significant experience answers: Where am I? What matters now? What can I do? What happens next?

Use progressive disclosure, contextual actions, overflow, drawers, bottom sheets, guided flows, search, smart prompts, automation and permission-aware options instead of permanent feature clutter.

## 3. Identity, CRM and communications
Use one identity with governed organization, role, workspace and relationship contexts. Do not duplicate people merely because they participate in multiple contexts.

Phone, mobile, email, SMS, calls, voice/voicemail, preferences and conversation history are shared identity infrastructure. Normalize/validate contact methods; deduplicate carefully; never auto-merge consequential records on weak fuzzy similarity.

Communication records resolve, where applicable: sender, recipient, person/contact, organization/workspace, portal/role context, request/lead/job/appointment, channel, consent/preference, direction, delivery/failure history, timestamps, source/assignment, workflow/automation provenance and audit.

CRM operates around work, not data entry. Records expose the next useful actions and progressively disclose identity, relationships, requests, opportunities, communications, appointments, notes, tasks, documents, agreements, jobs, attribution, assignments, consent, justified tags, relevant commercial data and audit.

Maintain an understandable activity timeline of material events without UI-event noise.

## 4. Attention and fast action
Maintain one coherent attention/work model for actionable items such as new requests, follow-ups, appointment confirmation, communications needing response, document/signature/verification/billing actions, workflow exceptions, escalations, expirations, failures, assignments, invitations and approvals.

Attention items define reason, priority, due state, responsible actor, source record, available action and completion/result.

From appropriate records/attention surfaces make Call, Text, Email, Reply, Follow up, Note, Schedule, Reschedule, Confirm, Cancel, Re-engage, Escalate and full history fast—especially on mobile.

## 5. Appointments, events and human deadlines
Appointments are governed workflow records, not isolated calendar entries. Support qualification, decision-maker requirements where applicable, availability/windows, assignment, confirmation/reminders, reschedule/cancel/no-show/missed/change reasons, notes, communications, follow-up/re-engagement and outcome. Reconcile calendar and CRM state.

Events/invitations define creation, audience, invitation, response/RSVP, reminders, changes, cancellation, attendance/no-show where relevant, follow-up, permissions, channels, calendar representation and resulting history.

Human-dependent workflows define responder, expected period, reminders, escalation, permitted reassignment, timeout behavior and safe AI/automation continuation. Distinguish normal waiting from stalled work.

Kendrell = HQ; Dion = Operations/BI; Diamond = Customer Experience. Use one universal launcher, one active panel and contextual capability. Full persona cards belong on dedicated agent surfaces. No competing rails/strips. Voice/microphone off by default unless newer authority changes it.
